# Code of conduct

Be respectful in issues, PRs, reviews, and other project spaces. Discuss the work and provide evidence. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or make personal attacks.

Respect contributors' time and privacy. Disagreement about a technical decision is welcome. Repeated pressure, abusive language, and retaliation are not.

[@eliophan](https://github.com/eliophan), the primary maintainer, handles moderation. The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation. The response depends on the behavior and its impact.

Report public misconduct with GitHub's Report content control. For a private report, use GitHub's reporting channels rather than posting personal details. Security vulnerabilities belong in the repository's [private vulnerability reporting channel](https://github.com/eliophan/t3code-bar/security/advisories/new), not a public conduct report. Reports are handled as time permits, without an SLA.
